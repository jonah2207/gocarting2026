The Circuit

Inspired by the legendary Big Kart Track layout, but scaled up and widened for true open-wheel racing. 1.2 kilometers of sweeping bends, aggressive hairpins, and a massive 400m straight where you can open the throttle wide.

Turn 1: The Sweeper

A high-speed right-hander. Trust the downforce, trust the slick tires, and keep your foot pinned.

The Hairpin

Heavy braking zone. Drop three gears with the paddle shift, hit the apex perfectly, and power out early.

Sector 3 Chicanes

Test your reaction times. Quick left-right transitions demanding absolute precision to maintain momentum.
